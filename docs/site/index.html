HWFWM Compendium

An unofficial Android fan reference for the magical system from He Who Fights With Monsters.

Browse essences, abilities, awakening stones, and status effects. Build character sheets from essence and confluence combinations. Roll random essence sets. Contribute your own entries that survive canonical data updates. Works fully offline. No ads. No analytics. No sign-in.

Source on GitHub Privacy policy Report an issue

What it does

  • Search & detail screens for essences, abilities, awakening stones, and status effects.
  • Character build helper — assemble essence/confluence sets and see the combined ability roster.
  • Contributions layer — add or correct user-supplied entries. Stored separately from canonical data, so canonical updates can never overwrite your work.
  • Backup & restore — export contributions as a JSON bundle or back them up to your own Google Drive.

Privacy at a glance

No analytics. No telemetry. No advertising. No account required. Everything lives on your device unless you explicitly opt into Google Drive backup, which writes only to your Drive.
