Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-15

This privacy policy applies to the HWFWM Compendium Android app (package name wizardry.compendium), an unofficial fan reference for the He Who Fights With Monsters magical system.

TL;DR. The app collects nothing. There are no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, and no user accounts. Your data stays on your device unless you choose to back it up to your own Google Drive.

Information the app does NOT collect

The app does not collect, process, transmit, store on remote servers, or share with third parties any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone, address).
  • Device identifiers (advertising ID, Android ID, IMEI, MAC address).
  • Location data of any kind.
  • Network identifiers, browsing data, or app activity.
  • Crash logs, diagnostic data, or analytics events.
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, or microphone audio.

The app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting SDKs, and no third-party trackers.

Information you create locally

The app stores the following data on your device only:

  • The canonical reference dataset that ships with the app (essences, abilities, awakening stones, etc.) — read-only, identical for every user.
  • Your contributions: additions or corrections you type into the app to supplement the canonical dataset.
  • App preferences (e.g. which data source is active, UI toggles).

This data is held in app-private SQLite databases and Android's DataStore. It is included in standard Android app backups if you have those enabled at the OS level; otherwise it is removed when you uninstall the app.

Optional: Google Drive backup

The app offers an opt-in feature to back up your contributions to your own Google Drive account.

  • Activation: you must explicitly enable this and grant Drive permission via Google's standard consent screen.
  • Scope: the app requests the minimum Drive scope needed to write into a dedicated folder it creates in your Drive.
  • Destination: the backup file lives in your Drive. It is not transmitted to any server operated by the developer.
  • Contents: only the user-contributed entries you authored in the app. The canonical dataset is not included (it ships with the app already).
  • Revocation: revoke the app's Drive access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the backup file directly from Drive.

Optional: export / import

The app's "Export contributions" feature creates a JSON file that you can share via Android's share sheet (email, messaging, file storage, etc.). This is initiated by you, the file goes wherever you send it, and the developer never receives a copy.

Children's privacy

This app is intended for adult fans of the book series and is not directed at children under 13. The app collects no personal information from any user, child or otherwise.

Permissions the app declares

  • INTERNET — required only by the optional Google Drive backup flow. When Drive backup is disabled, the app does not make network requests.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted at the same URL with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after such a change constitutes acceptance.
